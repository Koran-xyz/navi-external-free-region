# Paper Outline

## 1. Abstract
Problem / method or proposal / main finding or contribution / limitation.

## 2. Introduction
- Problem
- Why it matters
- Research question
- Contribution

## 3. Related Work
- Closest prior work
- Similarity
- Difference
- Gap addressed here

## 4. Method / Proposed Framework
Definitions, architecture, procedure, comparison conditions.

## 5. Results / Analysis
Only observed or measured results belong here.

## 6. Discussion
Interpretation, alternative explanations, generalizability.

## 7. Limitations
Actual limitations, not ceremonial caveats.

## 8. Conclusion
What is supported; what remains open.
