# Pre-submission Review Checklist

- [ ] Research question is explicit.
- [ ] Contribution differs from prior work in substance, not naming alone.
- [ ] Every factual citation is verified or marked unverified.
- [ ] No invented DOI, URL, author, quote, page, or result.
- [ ] Hypotheses are not written as findings.
- [ ] User observations are distinguished from independently verified evidence.
- [ ] Causal language matches the method.
- [ ] Negative/contrary evidence was searched for.
- [ ] Alternative explanations are discussed.
- [ ] Limitations are specific.
- [ ] Reproducibility details are sufficient for the paper type.
- [ ] Confidential/patent-sensitive material is cleared.
- [ ] Professor approved the final candidate.
